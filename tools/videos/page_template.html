How it was built

  1. One queue. All posts live in one file, written to a fixed case-study format and checked by tests before anything goes out.
  2. A content guard. Every post is checked before publishing: no unfinished placeholders, broken formatting or posts over LinkedIn's limit.
  3. Real demos, not mock-ups. Each video captures the live product frame by frame on desktop and mobile, with captions taken from the product itself and original music.
  4. Every 6 hours. A scheduled workflow publishes the next post through LinkedIn's official API, and never posts twice in one slot.
  5. Built with Claude. Writing, fact-checking against each product, the videos and the workflow were built with Claude, Anthropic's AI.
